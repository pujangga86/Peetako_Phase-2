Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Peetako is designed to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number or age.

What we store

  • Your pet: its name, type, stats, tier and timestamps, plus its public Call Code.
  • A hash of your Secret Key: a one-way scrambled form. We cannot turn it back into your key.
  • Session records: a hash of the session token for each device you choose to remember, with an expiry date.
  • Care alerts (only if you turn them on): the push address your browser gives us, the encryption keys that go with it, and your device's time zone (so we stay quiet at night). We also record which alerts your pet has already had, so each is sent once. This is removed when you turn alerts off, tap "Leave this device", or your pet falls.
  • Rate-limit counters: short-lived counters keyed by a hashed form of your IP address, used to stop abuse. They are deleted automatically once they expire.

Cookies

We set one strictly necessary cookie, ptk_session, so the game knows which pet you are caring for. It is HttpOnly (scripts cannot read it) and lasts until you close the browser, or 30 days if you tick "Remember this device". Leaving the device deletes it.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the game and its database. Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile may be used when hatching a pet, to check that you are human.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on the site.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your device) delivers care alerts if you turn them on. Alerts are encrypted so only your device can read them.

If we add advertising in future, this page will be updated first to explain what the advertiser collects and how to opt out.

Children

Peetako is suitable for all ages. Because there is no chat and no personal data, players cannot share personal information through the game.

Deleting your data

A pet that is not cared for falls after its survival window. If you want a pet removed sooner, contact us with its Call Code.
