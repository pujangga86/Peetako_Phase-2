About Peetako

A small game with a Malaysian Borneo heart.

Peetako was created by a developer who was born in Sarawak and raised in Sabah. The name grows out of a teenage nickname, "Pitak", given for a square face — which is why every Peetako has a rounded-square face too.

Why a gecko?

Geckos are everywhere in Malaysian Borneo: on the walls of every house, calling chik-chak at night. The English word "gecko" itself comes from Malay. Borneo is also home to real flying geckos, which is why an Organic Peetako learns to glide when it becomes a Hero.

Our approach to culture

Place names, wildlife, food and music from Malaysian Borneo (Sabah and Sarawak) appear freely in the game. Patterns and costumes are inspired by traditional designs from the Iban, Orang Ulu and Kadazandusun communities, never copied from specific pieces. Sacred and ritual elements are left out unless the communities themselves agree they can be used. We plan to credit cultural reviewers here.

What's built so far

This is an early version. You can hatch a pet and choose its colour (once, at hatch), play in a full-screen or retro-framed view, care for it, watch its mood and its sky change, see it age through tiers, choose Ascension at the top tier, and — if you forget it — see it fall. Meeting other pets by Call Code, battles, rankings and seasonal events are coming in later updates.
