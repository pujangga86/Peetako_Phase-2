What is Peetako?

Peetako is a free virtual pet you raise in your browser. Your pet is a cute gecko with a rounded-square face, and it comes in two kinds: an Organic rainforest gecko, or a Robot gecko built in a riverside workshop. Its story, places and food all come from Malaysian Borneo — Sabah and Sarawak.

There is no account and no email. When your Peetako hatches you get a Secret Key of four random words. That key is the only thing you need to remember. Lose it, and nobody can care for your pet again.

How to play

  1. Hatch an egg and pick Organic or Robot.
  2. Save your four-word Secret Key.
  3. Come back regularly to feed, clean, warm (or cool) and play with your gecko.
  4. Watch it climb tiers as it ages — from Hatchling to Ageless in about two months.

Age is power

A Peetako has no maximum lifespan. The older it gets, the higher its tier, and the longer it can survive on its own: a Hatchling lasts two days without care, while an Ageless Peetako (Day 60) lasts two weeks. If the survival window runs out, it falls and joins the Hall of Legends. After a month as Ageless, you may also choose Ascension: retire your Peetako to the Hall of Legends with a Legacy Score.

Born in Malaysian Borneo

Organic Peetako eat kelkatu (termite alates) and bask under a lamp because geckos are cold-blooded. Robot Peetako charge on Hydro Cells powered by the Rajang River and need coolant to stop overheating. As your pet grows, its backdrop travels across Malaysian Borneo: a longhouse, a kampung garden, the sago gardens of Mukah, the rainforest of Danum and Bako, the fireflies of the Kinabatangan, the bat exodus at Mulu, the sea at Semporna, the Bario highlands and finally the summit of Kinabalu.

Your Peetako shows how it feels: a smile and floating hearts when it is happy, a thought bubble when it is hungry, Z letters when it is sleepy and a little rain cloud when it is sad. Its sky follows your own clock, from dawn to night.

Fun fact: the English word "gecko" comes from Malay — it imitates the lizard's own call, chik-chak.
