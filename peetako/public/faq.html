Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how Peetako works.

Is Peetako free?
Yes. Peetako is free to play in any modern browser, on phone or computer.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Each pet has a four-word Secret Key, and that key is how you get back to it.
I lost my Secret Key. Can you recover it?
No. We only store a scrambled (hashed) version of the key, so nobody — including us — can read it back. If this device still remembers your pet, you can keep playing here, but the key cannot be shown again. If you lose both, the pet will eventually fall from neglect. You can always hatch a new one.
What does "Remember this device" do?
It keeps a secure session cookie on this browser for 30 days, so you don't have to type your key every visit. The key itself is never stored on your device. If you untick it, the session ends when you close the browser (or after 12 hours).
Can I install Peetako as an app?
Yes. On Android, or in Chrome or Edge on a computer, tap Install app at the bottom of the game page (or use your browser's own install option). On iPhone and iPad, open Peetako in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On iPhone and iPad the installed app keeps its own storage, so enter your Secret Key once inside the app and tick "Remember this device". Caring for your pet still needs an internet connection.
Can Peetako remind me to care for my pet?
Yes. Tap the bell in the corner of your pet's scene and allow notifications. You will get an alert when your pet goes dormant, when about 12 hours of its survival window are left, a last-chance alert when about 3 hours are left, and a note when it reaches a new tier. Alerts are quiet from 22:00 to 08:00 on your device, except the last-chance alert. On iPhone and iPad, alerts only work after you add Peetako to your Home Screen and open it from there. Tap the bell again to turn alerts off.
How often do I need to visit?
Stats drop slowly over time. A young Peetako survives about two days without any care; older tiers survive longer (up to 14 days at Tier 8). Any care action resets the timer.
What happens when my pet is "Dormant"?
After 24 hours without care, your Peetako goes dormant and sleeps. It is still alive — care for it and it wakes up straight away.

What happens after Tier 8?
There is still no maximum lifespan: your Ageless Peetako can keep living as long as you care for it. After 30 days as Ageless (Day 90), its age stops counting for rankings and power, and you may choose Ascension: it retires to the Hall of Legends with a Legacy Score, made of the days it lived plus points for every day you cared for it. Ascension is optional and cannot be undone. Only an active Peetako (cared for in the last 24 hours) can ascend.
How do I know how my Peetako feels?
Watch its face and the symbol beside it. The four buttons also show a ! when the need they look after drops below 40. Floating hearts mean it is happy, a thought bubble means it is hungry, Z letters mean it is sleepy, a rain cloud means it is sad, and sparkles mean it is excited — usually right after you care for it. A small label under its name says the same in words.
Can I change how the game looks?
Yes. Tap the menu (☰) on your pet's screen. View switches between Immersive (the scene fills the whole screen, with the controls in a dock at the bottom) and Classic (everything inside a retro frame). Your choice is saved on this device only. Your Peetako's colour is chosen once, when you hatch it, from five palettes: Hijau Rimba, Tokay, Kenyalang, Pua Kumbu and Laut Borneo. It cannot be changed afterwards. Colours never change how your pet plays.
Does Peetako make sounds?
Only if you want it to. Sound is off by default; turn it on from the menu. The sounds are short and quiet, made by your browser, and the choice is saved on this device.
Why does the sky change?
The sky follows the clock on your device: dawn, day, dusk and night. It is only for looks — your pet's survival timer always runs on the server's clock, so changing your device time changes nothing in the game.
What is the Call Code?
A short public code like PTK-4K7Q9M. It is safe to share. In a later update, other players will be able to use it to visit, pamper and challenge your Peetako.
Can I chat with other players?
No. Peetako has no free-text chat — only preset actions — to keep it safe for players of all ages.
Can I have more than one Peetako?
Yes. Each pet has its own Secret Key. To limit spam, only a few pets can be hatched per network each day.
